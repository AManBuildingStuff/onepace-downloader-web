Setup tips (one-time)

  1. Let magnet links open Transmission. Open Transmission → Settings → General, and make it the default app for magnet links. The first time you click Open, your browser asks to allow it. In Chrome, tick “Always allow”.
  2. Start downloads without a prompt. Transmission → Settings → Transfers: turn off “Display a window when opening a torrent file” and pick a download folder.
  3. Batch sends. “Send to torrent client” opens the magnets one at a time. If your browser blocks some of them, use the queue that pops up, or Copy links and paste them into your client.
  4. Direct (Pixeldrain) not loading? Some ISPs, Airtel and Jio among them, block pixeldrain.com and nyaa.si at the DNS level. To fix that, go to System Settings → Network → (your Wi-Fi) → Details → DNS and add 1.1.1.1 and 1.0.0.1. Torrents (magnets) still work without this, because your client talks to trackers and peers directly.
  5. Pixeldrain limits. Free Pixeldrain downloads are rate-limited per day. For a big arc like Wano, torrents are usually the better route.
